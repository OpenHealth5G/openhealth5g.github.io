The Research Team Behind OpenHealth5G

Research Team

Prof. Dr. Juliano Araujo Wickboldt

General Coordinator

CNPq research fellow (Level C) and Associate Professor at UFRGS Institute of Computing. Leads the OpenHealth5G project across all partner institutions, with research focus on 5G/6G technologies, network slicing, software-defined and programmable networks, edge computing, digital twins, and distributed systems. Previously conducted post-doctoral research in converged optical/wireless networks under the EU-Brazil FUTEBOL (H2020) project.

Prof. Dr. Muriel Figueredo Franco

Local Coordinator

Assistant Professor of Computer Science and Biomedical Informatics at UFCSPA and member of the Digital Health Hub. Ph.D. (Summa Cum Laude, 2023) in Computer Science from the University of Zurich. Research spans cybersecurity, computer networks, blockchain, AI, and health applications. Previously participated in RNP working groups on virtualized network functions (GT-FENDE) and cyberattack economics (GT-IMPACTO), and the H2020 CONCORDIA and FAPESP PROFISSA projects.

Prof. Dr. Alexandre Huff

Collaborator

Professor at UTFPR since 2013. Active in the O-RAN Software Community since 2019, maintaining official repositories including RIC Message Router (RMR), Traffic Steering xApp, xApp Frame CPP, and Cell Configuration and Control xApp. Research focuses on Near-Real-Time RIC development, E2SM-KPM and E2SM-RC models, and Open RAN simulation with E2Sim. Previously conducted sandwich doctorate research at AT&T (USA) with collaborations at Virginia Tech.
