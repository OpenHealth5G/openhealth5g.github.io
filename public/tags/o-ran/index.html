Network Slicing for Ultra-Reliable Emergency Telehealth in Open RAN Environments

This paper presents a novel network slicing architecture designed specifically for ultra-reliable low-latency communication (uRLLC) in emergency telehealth scenarios over Open RAN deployments. We demonstrate through the OpenRAN@Brasil testbed that dedicated uRLLC slices can achieve sub-5ms end-to-end latency for critical medical data streams while maintaining isolation from best-effort XR education traffic.
