# OpenHealth5G | Brand Identity & Design Guidelines

This document contains official branding guidelines for the **OpenHealth5G: Open 5G Networking for Digital Health** research project. This guide ensures that all research dissemination—from academic papers to social media—maintains a consistent, innovative, and tech-forward visual identity.

## 1. The Logo
The OpenHealth5G logo combines a heart (representing healthcare) with wireless signal bars (representing 5G connectivity). The graphic icon is on the left and stylized OpenHealth5G text on the right.

**1. The Icon:**
* **Shape:** On the far left is a stylized, thick-lined heart shape. The right side of the heart is not a closed loop; instead, it transitions into three curved, concentric arcs of varying thicknesses. These arcs resemble wireless signal waves (like a Wi-Fi or cellular signal icon) radiating outward from the heart.
* **Color:** The main body of the heart is a deep navy blue. The signal waves transition in color from a medium blue to a bright, vibrant cyan or teal at the outermost edge.

**2. The Typography:**
* **Text:** The text reads "OpenHealth5G" in a clean, sans-serif font.
* **Style:** The lettering uses "CamelCase," where the "O" in Open and the "H" in Health are capitalized. 
* **Color Coordination:** The "OpenHealth" portion of the text is the same deep navy blue as the heart. The "5G" portion is rendered in the same bright cyan/teal color found at the tip of the signal waves.

### Usage Guidelines:
* **Primary Logo:** Used on light/white backgrounds.
* **Negative/Dark Version:** Used on dark blue or black backgrounds to ensure legibility.
* **Monochrome:** Reserved for black-and-white printing (e.g., specific journal requirements).
* **Clearance:** Maintain a "safe zone" around the logo equal to the width of the "O" in the text.

## 2. Color Palette
Our colors reflect a mix of clinical reliability and cutting-edge technology.

| Color | Hex Code | Usage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Dark Blue** | `#004182` | Primary headings, logo text, dark backgrounds |
| **Light Blue** | `#00B8E6` | Accents, wireless bars, call-to-actions |
| **Soft White** | `#F4F7F9` | Section backgrounds, secondary text on dark |
| **Dark Slate** | `#333333` | Primary body text for high readability |

---

## 3. Typography
The **Ubuntu** font family is our primary typeface. It is chosen for its modern, humanist feel that aligns with open-source values.

* **Headings:** Ubuntu Bold (`#004182`)
* **Subheadings:** Ubuntu Medium (`#00B8E6`)
* **Body Text:** Ubuntu Regular (`#333333`)
* **Technical Labels:** Ubuntu Condensed

*Note: For LaTeX/Beamer users, please use the `ubuntu` package or the TrueType files found in the `/fonts` directory.*

---

## 4. Visual Style & Use Cases
Dissemination material should utilize the flat, vector-based illustration style established in our primary use-case diagrams:

1.  **Remote Emergency Telehealth:** Showcasing low-latency 5G connectivity.
2.  **Immersive Healthcare Education:** Focusing on VR/AR and high-bandwidth scenarios.

**Guideline:** When creating new diagrams (e.g., O-RAN architectures or Kubernetes deployments), please use the brand palette (Dark Blue and Light Blue) to maintain visual continuity.
