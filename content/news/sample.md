The OpenHealth5G consortium, comprising researchers from UFRGS, UFCSPA, UNISINOS, and PUCRS, has received a collaborative grant from the Sao Paulo Research Foundation (FAPESP) to support the deployment of a real-world emergency telehealth pilot program.

The pilot will utilize the OpenRAN@Brasil experimental infrastructure, leveraging 5G network slicing to prioritize ultra-low-latency video and sensor data transmission from first-responder teams to reference hospitals.

Key objectives include validating the N3IWF protocol for connecting portable medical devices to the 5G core, and measuring end-to-end latency across the distributed testbed connecting all four partner institutions via the Metropoa metropolitan network.
